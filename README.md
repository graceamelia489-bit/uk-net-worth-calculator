# UK Net Worth Calculator

A free, open-source net worth calculator for people in the UK. Enter what you own and what you owe, and it shows your net worth in GBP. It also shows the figure without your pension and without your home equity.

**Live calculator:** https://graceamelia489-bit.github.io/uk-net-worth-calculator/

Everything runs in your browser. No sign-up, no tracking, and nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

## How to calculate your net worth in the UK

**Net worth = total assets - total liabilities**

**Assets (what you own)**
- Current accounts, savings accounts and Premium Bonds
- ISAs: Cash ISA, Stocks & Shares ISA, Lifetime ISA
- Pensions: workplace pensions and SIPPs (use your latest statements)
- Your home and any other property, at a realistic current value (sold prices nearby are a better guide than asking prices)
- Other investments, crypto at today's price, and your car at its resale value

**Liabilities (what you owe)**
- Mortgage balance(s)
- Credit cards, personal loans and overdrafts
- Car finance (use the PCP/HP settlement figure)
- Student loan (optional, since many people leave Plan 2 loans out because they are written off)

## Tips

- Value assets at what you could sell them for today, not what you paid.
- Look at your net worth with and without your pension and home equity. The figure without them is the money you can actually use.
- Recalculate on the same day each month or quarter. The trend matters more than any single number.

## Automatic tracking

This calculator is a one-off snapshot. If you want your net worth tracked over time with real-time valuations for property, cars and crypto, see [Networthy](https://www.networthy.ai/).

## Run it yourself

It is a single `index.html` file with no dependencies. Download it and open it in any browser, or fork this repo.
